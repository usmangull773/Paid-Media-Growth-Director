01

What we found

Inno Supps already has the hard part: a team producing VSLs, AI video ads, static creative and landing pages at volume. The gap is a single system that decides which of those assets get budget, how fast, and on which channel.

Creative volume is ahead of creative testing

High impact

With several editors producing ads in parallel, the number of new assets grows faster than the account can test them fairly. Without fixed kill and scale rules, winners get found late and good concepts die on small budgets.

Without a testing system

  • New ads launched into mixed ad sets
  • Spend decided by gut on day two
  • Editors get vague feedback
  • Winning hooks are not reused

With a testing system

  • Fixed test cells per concept and hook
  • Kill and scale rules set by target CPA
  • Weekly creative scorecard for editors
  • Winners moved into scaling campaigns

Reporting is built by hand

High impact

Full funnel tracking has been handled through manual data entry. That means budget decisions run on yesterday's spreadsheet instead of live, matched numbers across ad platforms, the store and email.

The 30 day plan

Four weeks, sequenced so tracking is fixed before budget moves, and budget moves before scale.

90 day roadmap
1

Account and tracking audit

Review every ad account, pixel, Conversions API setup, GA4 and store data. Match platform purchases to real orders and set one source of truth for CPA and MER.

Why: every later decision depends on numbers you can trust.
2

Creative testing system

Build a test structure by concept, hook and format, with clear kill and scale thresholds. Give the editing team a weekly scorecard showing which hooks, openers and lengths win.

Why: turns your editors' volume into a steady supply of proven ads.
3

Budget reallocation by product and channel

Set target CPA and MER per hero product. Move budget toward proven winners on Meta, then add Google Search, Shopping and YouTube for VSL traffic where demand already exists.

Why: spend follows profit, not habit.
4

Funnel and offer tests

Run one test at a time on the step losing the most buyers: advertorial angle, offer bundle, or checkout upsell. Each test gets a clear win metric before it starts.

Why: a better conversion rate lowers CPA on every channel at once.
5

Reporting cadence and 90 day roadmap

Daily dashboard, weekly performance review with the team, and a written 90 day plan for scaling spend with clear CPA guardrails.

Why: leadership sees the same numbers the media buyer sees.
